Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 4, 2026

This policy explains how Rakjai ("we", "the app") collects, uses and protects your personal data. Rakjai is a mobile application for discovering and tracking BL & GL series, currently in development.

Who is responsible for your data

DAKIBEN Software & Quality Engineering, located in Barranquilla, Colombia, is the data controller. For any question about this policy or about your data, contact rakjaiapp@gmail.com.

What we collect

When you create an account using Google or Facebook, we receive:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your profile picture, when available
  • The unique identifier assigned to you by that provider

We never receive or store your password for those services. During registration we also ask you to provide your country and date of birth, and to accept our terms of service.

While you use the app, we store the content you create:

  • Series you follow or mark as watched
  • Episode progress
  • Your preferences and lists

How we use it

  • To create and maintain your account
  • To sync your tracking data across devices
  • To send transactional emails, such as account verification and password recovery
  • To keep the service secure and prevent abuse

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Services we rely on

  • MongoDB Atlas — stores your account and tracking data
  • Resend — delivers transactional emails
  • Google and Meta — provide sign-in, only when you choose to use them
  • The Movie Database (TMDB) — provides series information shown in the app

These providers process data on our behalf and may store it outside Colombia, including in the United States and the European Union. By using Rakjai you consent to this international transfer, as provided under Colombian Law 1581 of 2012.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is active. If you delete your account, we remove your personal data within 30 days, except where we are required to retain it by law.

Your rights

You can access, correct, update or delete your personal data, and withdraw your consent at any time. To delete your account, use the option in the app settings or write to rakjaiapp@gmail.com. We respond to requests within 15 business days.

Age requirement

Rakjai is intended for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If we learn that we have collected data from someone under 18, we delete it.

Security

We use encrypted connections, access controls and hashed credentials to protect your data. No system is completely secure, but we work to keep risk to a minimum.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we will update the date at the top of this page and notify you in the app.
